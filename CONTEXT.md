# Product framing skills

Skills that review or build early product artifacts (a problem statement, a persona, an MVP
scope) against criteria taken from published sources.

## Language

**Source**:
A published page a skill cites by tag, such as [PD]. Every criterion rests on one.
_Avoid_: reading, reference, material

**Criterion**:
One row of a skill's Criteria table: what to look for in the user's text, and the source that
says so.
_Avoid_: rule, check, requirement

**Guidelines**:
What the user is writing to, as stated in their request, such as a length or a template.
_Avoid_: brief, course spec, rubric, requirement
